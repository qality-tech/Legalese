# The qality.tech Discord bot — a plain-language notice

**Last changed:** 20 Nov 2026
**Who we are:** the people who run the **qality.tech** Discord server — the members of its `@Owners`
role. Discord handle `settlersxp` is one of us. **We are not a company.** There is no legal entity
here, no service being sold, no contract, no fees, and no terms of service. This is an experiment:
we wanted to see what a chatbot could do with the conversations a small team already has.

**Where things stand right now:** the bot is **not reading anything yet**. It is installed in one
server and configured, but it has not been switched on. This page describes what it will do when we
turn it on, written before we did, so that nobody finds out afterwards.

## What the bot does

A Discord application named `wiki-bot` (application ID `1573304492886204447`) watches **one category**
of text channels in our own server, and copies what it finds into a wiki that we run ourselves: a
thread becomes a page, and a command like `/decision` becomes a dated line saying what was decided
and who recorded it. Every entry links back to the Discord message it came from.

## What that means if you post in that category

- Your message text is **copied somewhere else and stays there** until one of us deletes it.
- Your **Discord display name and user ID** are recorded alongside anything you file, so a decision
  has a name attached to it.
- Treat **nothing** in that category as confidential. Not because we would misuse it, but because
  copying it to a second place is exactly what the thing is for.
- If that is not what you signed up for: say so. We will take the channel out of the category, or
  delete the page, or switch the whole thing off. That is not a request you have to justify.

## What it does not do

It has no access to direct messages, voice audio, voice channel membership, or online status, and it
does not browse the member list. Its only server permissions are **View Channels**, **Send Messages**
and **Read Message History** — no administrator, no moderation, nothing that could delete or
rearrange the server. It has no website, no accounts, no analytics, no cookies and no tracking of any
kind. Nothing we collect is sold, used for advertising, or used to train a model.

## Who else could see it

1. **Discord**, as the platform the bot talks through, under Discord's own terms.
2. **Our own wiki**, where filed pages live, and which only accounts we have given access can read.
3. **A text-summarising service, if we ever switch that part on.** It would mean sending message text
   to a third-party provider to draft a summary. As of the date above this is **not configured and
   not in use**, and we will add the provider's name to this page before any of it is switched on.

## Where it runs, and for how long

On a server we operate ourselves, in **[COUNTRY — fill in]**, reachable only from inside our own
network. It keeps no archive of messages: the only thing stored on that machine is a small file
mapping channel to wiki folder, which contains no message text. Wiki pages stay until we delete them.
Deleting the original Discord message, or the page, removes the content from that place.

## Taking it away

Any server owner can remove the bot at any time — Server Settings → Members → `wiki-bot` → **Kick** —
and that stops all further collection immediately, without asking anyone.

We are testing, and what we test next may read more, or store differently, or connect to something
new. When it does, this page changes with it, and the date at the top changes. It will not be
replaced quietly and left to speak for an older bot.
